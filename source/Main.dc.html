About us

ABC After School Program gives children in K to Grade 8 a safe, friendly place to learn and play once the school day ends. We offer daily care, academic support and enrichment activities that go beyond the classroom.

Child safety

Every child is signed in and out by an approved adult. Our staff are background checked and trained in first aid, and activity spaces are supervised at all times.

Our staff

Our team includes qualified teachers and activity leaders who help with homework, run projects and make learning fun.

Winter Break

Supervised full days while school is closed. Children enjoy science experiments, art, reading circles, outdoor games and a little revision, so learning keeps going through the holidays. Open to K to Grade 8.
